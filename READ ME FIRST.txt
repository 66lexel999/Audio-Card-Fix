FOCUSRITE DOCTOR
Fixes "Cannot open Focusrite USB ASIO. (Error code: 0x54f)" in TONEX / AmpliTube.

1. Right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" if you see it > OK.
2. Right-click the zip > Extract All > Extract.
3. Plug in the Focusrite and CLOSE TONEX / AmpliTube.
4. In the extracted folder, double-click  Run-Focusrite-Doctor.bat
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".
   - Click "Yes" when Windows asks for Administrator permission.
5. Answer the questions: Y = yes, N = no, Enter = the suggested answer.
   Nothing on your PC is changed unless you answer Y.
6. At the end, the report is saved on your Desktop AND copied to the clipboard.
   Paste it (Ctrl+V) into WhatsApp / Discord and send it back.

The tool only uses tools that are built into Windows. You can open
Focusrite-Doctor.ps1 in Notepad to read exactly what it does.
